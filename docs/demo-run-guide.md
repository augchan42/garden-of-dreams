# Garden of Dreams: first-reading demo

**Build:** `GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck`, exported with Godot 4.7.2 Standard for macOS. Use the installed `/Applications/Godot.app`. Double-click `Run Garden of Dreams.command` beside the pack, or run `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --main-pack GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck` from a terminal. The 43-second `GardenOfDreamsDemo.mp4` shows the route. The demo works offline and does not sign in or contact the reading service.

**Play:** Read the green terminal, choose **Enter the garden**, then **Follow the lanterns**. At Qinfang Pavilion, choose **Examine the table** and **Cast at the table**. Close the result card, choose **Finish the demo**, then **Begin again** to replay. Buttons work with a mouse or keyboard focus and Enter. The command field is optional. A typical first visit takes about four minutes at a reading pace; the scripted capture is shorter.

**Color:** The green grade is set to 20% strength. The stage key lights and painted sky are less saturated, while CRTs and lanterns retain their local colors. Change `shader_parameter/grade_strength` in `godot/runtime/color_grade.tscn` to adjust the remaining grade (0 is ungraded; 1 is the original grade).

**What is included:** The three-coin cast produces six 6/7/8/9 outcomes locally. The six bronze lines show the primary hexagram; the card identifies moving lines and any transformed hexagram. It does not provide AI interpretation or private history. The wider 14-location garden is available in the repository scene `godot/runtime/entry_route.tscn`, outside this focused pack.

**Validation:** A clean Godot import, cold desktop launch of the exported pack, and uninterrupted mouse/keyboard cell-to-replay tests from both project and pack passed on an Apple M2 Max. At 1410 × 600, three stationary views measured 85–126 visible draw calls, 122,636–157,702 visible primitives, and 65,462,456 bytes of texture allocation. Forced-draw intervals had medians of 1.32–3.84 ms and p95 of 2.94–7.13 ms in this run. The capture and 390 × 844 screenshots were inspected. This is macOS desktop evidence; a mid-range phone GPU and other desktop hardware have not been tested.
