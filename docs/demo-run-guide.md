# Garden of Dreams: first-reading demo

**Build:** `GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck`, exported with Godot 4.7.2 Standard for macOS. Use the installed `/Applications/Godot.app`. Double-click `Run Garden of Dreams.command` beside the pack, or run `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --main-pack GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck` from a terminal. The 43-second `GardenOfDreamsDemo.mp4` shows the route. The demo works offline and does not sign in or contact the reading service.

**Play:** Read the green terminal, choose **Enter the garden**, then **Follow the lanterns**. At Qinfang Pavilion, choose **Examine the table** and **Cast at the table**. Close the result card, choose **Finish the demo**, then **Begin again** to replay. Buttons work with a mouse or keyboard focus and Enter. The command field is optional. A typical first visit takes about four minutes at a reading pace; the scripted capture is shorter.

**Color:** The green grade is set to 20% strength. The stage key lights and painted sky are less saturated, while CRTs and lanterns retain their local colors. Change `shader_parameter/grade_strength` in `godot/runtime/color_grade.tscn` to adjust the remaining grade (0 is ungraded; 1 is the original grade).

**What is included:** The three-coin cast produces six 6/7/8/9 outcomes locally. The six bronze lines show the primary hexagram; the card identifies moving lines and any transformed hexagram. It does not provide AI interpretation or private history. The wider 14-location garden is available in the repository scene `godot/runtime/entry_route.tscn`, outside this focused pack.

**Validation:** Asset reimport, cold desktop launch of the exported pack, and uninterrupted mouse/keyboard cell-to-replay tests from both project and pack passed on an Apple M2 Max. At 1410 × 600, three stationary views measured 101–145 visible draw calls, 123,148–146,432 visible primitives, and 66,544,800 bytes (63.46 MiB) of texture allocation. Forced-draw intervals had medians of 1.31–1.97 ms and p95 of 2.22–3.15 ms in this run. The capture and 390 × 844 screenshots were inspected. This is macOS desktop evidence; a mid-range phone GPU and other desktop hardware have not been tested.

**Asset provenance:** The editable Blender sites and assembly are in `blender/`; the canonical glTF export is `export/garden-of-dreams.glb` (SHA256 `562b72dd698ed75504b98c30f8860a0ab24a81dc6de800b8b5b0247e82f40c2a`). The shared flora kit adds muted foliage, blossoms and bark; its original 2048px atlas is imported at 512px with mipmaps and compression for the runtime texture budget. The shared prop kit adds warm paper lanterns and cream stone stools in two demo-site batches. Its original PBR maps are imported at 512/256/256px, and small dressing batches stop drawing beyond 28 metres. The 32 demo lightmaps were baked from that file with `scripts/bake_lightmaps.py --site demo` and are indexed by `godot/lightmaps/demo-index.json`; the transparent gate inscription retains its source material. The six sound cues are original seeded synthesis described in `godot/audio/demo/README.md`. Pavilion and wall atlases are original procedural art described in `textures/atlases/pavilion/README.md` and `textures/atlases/wall/README.md`. The painted farmhouse backdrop and gate inscription were generated for this project; provenance is in `textures/backdrops/README.md` and `textures/decals/gate-inscription-provenance.md`. The 64-hexagram pattern catalog comes from the sibling 8-Bit Oracle source documented in `godot/data/README.md`.
