# Garden of Dreams: first-reading demo

**Build:** `GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck`, exported with Godot 4.7.2 Standard for macOS. Use the installed `/Applications/Godot.app`. Double-click `Run Garden of Dreams.command` beside the pack, or run `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --main-pack GardenOfDreamsDemo.pck` from a terminal. The 43-second `GardenOfDreamsDemo.mp4` shows the route. The demo works offline and does not sign in or contact the reading service.

**Play:** Read the green terminal, choose **Enter the garden**, then **Follow the lanterns**. At Qinfang Pavilion, choose **Examine the table** and **Cast at the table**. Close the result card, choose **Finish the demo**, then **Begin again** to replay. Buttons work with a mouse or keyboard focus and Enter. The command field is optional. A typical first visit takes about four minutes at a reading pace; the scripted capture is shorter.

**Color:** The green grade is set to 20% strength. The stage key lights and painted sky are less saturated, while CRTs and lanterns retain their local colors. Change `shader_parameter/grade_strength` in `godot/runtime/color_grade.tscn` to adjust the remaining grade (0 is ungraded; 1 is the original grade).

**What is included:** The three-coin cast produces six 6/7/8/9 outcomes locally. The six bronze lines show the primary hexagram; the card identifies moving lines and any transformed hexagram. It does not provide AI interpretation or private history. The wider 14-location garden is available in the repository scene `godot/runtime/entry_route.tscn`, outside this focused pack.

**Validation:** Asset reimport, cold desktop launch of the exported pack, and uninterrupted mouse/keyboard cell-to-replay tests from both project and pack passed on an Apple M2 Max. At 1410 × 600, three stationary views measured 104–149 visible draw calls, 119,190–149,946 visible primitives, and 66,282,616 bytes (63.21 MiB) of texture allocation. Forced-draw intervals had medians of 1.82–2.06 ms and p95 of 3.89–4.48 ms in this run. The capture and 390 × 844 screenshots were inspected. This is macOS desktop evidence; a mid-range phone GPU and other desktop hardware have not been tested.

**Asset provenance:** The editable Blender sites and assembly are in `blender/`; the canonical glTF export is `export/garden-of-dreams.glb` (SHA256 `8b18e3263ba1bdb5f140e2790e3f5b0f19b1a6be677038c9defcee788c9b62ba`). The shared flora kit adds muted foliage, blossoms and bark; its original 2048px atlas is imported at 512px with mipmaps and compression for the runtime texture budget. The 32 demo lightmaps were baked from that file with `scripts/bake_lightmaps.py --site demo` and are indexed by `godot/lightmaps/demo-index.json`; the transparent gate inscription retains its source material. The six sound cues are original seeded synthesis described in `godot/audio/demo/README.md`. Pavilion and wall atlases are original procedural art described in `textures/atlases/pavilion/README.md` and `textures/atlases/wall/README.md`. The painted farmhouse backdrop and gate inscription were generated for this project; provenance is in `textures/backdrops/README.md` and `textures/decals/gate-inscription-provenance.md`. The 64-hexagram pattern catalog comes from the sibling 8-Bit Oracle source documented in `godot/data/README.md`.
