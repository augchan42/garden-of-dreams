# Site sheet: 瀟湘館

Slug `xiaoxiang-guan`. Priority 4. Function: Bamboo court dressing. Room id `xiaoxiang_guan`.

## Stage direction

Bamboo partly hides a quiet court. One window glows through the stems, while the entrance remains closed. Repeated cards should form layered silhouettes rather than a solid hedge.

## Dimensions and access

6 m facade; bamboo 2.5–4.5 m; narrow 1.8 m approach. Maintain a continuous collision-tested approach. Future rooms remain closed rather than exposing unfinished interiors.

## Assets

Bamboo in three sizes; closed court gate; one lit window; small stone path. Reuse stock corridor, wall, roof, lantern and stage materials where their silhouettes fit this site; do not force every site into the same hall mesh.

## Lighting and atmosphere

Green side key through bamboo; one amber window. Exterior floor fog stays between 0.3 and 0.8 m. Keep the walking surface and interactable furniture readable. Unfinished backs use MAT_backstage.

## Room markers

Bamboo approach; closed gate; return path. Each marker is a named `TRG_` empty carrying the room id. The existing `TRG_xiaoxiang_guan_entry` is the initial marker; add specific action markers during the site pass. Game actions belong in Godot.

## Camera contract

Compose through a gap between bamboo clumps with the window as the focal point. Ship a 2.35:1 establishing camera and a constrained visitor rail. Check both approach and return views for exposed backs or intersections.

## References to collect

1. A sourced Shaw Brothers night-set still for the lighting and built-set treatment.
2. A sourced architectural reference specific to this site's structure.
3. A sourced close view of its distinguishing material or foliage.

These are collection requirements, not claims that reference stills have been collected.

## Current build and acceptance

The generic hall has been replaced by an east-facing six-metre facade with a closed paired timber gate, one amber window and a single shallow pitched roof. Twelve bamboo stalks in 2.5, 3.5 and 4.5 m sizes form four separate clumps around a 1.8 m stone aisle. Four markers and three cameras are exported. Qinfang’s “Visit the bamboo court” action connects the southern path.

Desktop and portrait renders are generated by `godot/tests/render_bamboo.gd`. Painted signage, sourced reference images, foliage refinement and final lighting/bakes remain unfinished; dynamic shadow striping is visible on the wall.

Acceptance: distinct site silhouette, complete specified props, named markers, traversable approach, no exposed unfinished backs on the camera rail, and one graded reference render matching this sheet. The stage-wide first pass is not evidence of completion for this site.
