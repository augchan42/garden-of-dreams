## Stage direction

A closed lacquered court sits east of the central pavilion. The colour comes from the lacquer under the restricted lighting; the door remains visibly closed.

## Dimensions and access

7 m frontage; 2.1 m door head; shallow black-backed interior. Maintain a continuous collision-tested approach. Future rooms remain closed rather than exposing unfinished interiors.

## Assets

Paired lacquer doors; banana leaves; stone threshold; wall cap; one lit window. Reuse stock corridor, wall, roof, lantern and stage materials where their silhouettes fit this site; do not force every site into the same hall mesh.

## Lighting and atmosphere

Amber from one window; green hard key on leaves and roof. Exterior floor fog stays between 0.3 and 0.8 m. Keep the walking surface and interactable furniture readable. Unfinished backs use MAT_backstage.
