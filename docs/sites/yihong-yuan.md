# Site sheet: 怡紅院

Slug `yihong-yuan`. Priority 4. Function: Future room dressing. Room id `yihong_yuan`.

## Stage direction

A closed lacquered court sits east of the central pavilion. The colour comes from the lacquer under the restricted lighting; the door remains visibly closed.

## Dimensions and access

7 m frontage; 2.1 m door head; shallow black-backed interior. Maintain a continuous collision-tested approach. Future rooms remain closed rather than exposing unfinished interiors.

## Assets

Paired lacquer doors; banana leaves; stone threshold; wall cap; one lit window. Reuse stock corridor, wall, roof, lantern and stage materials where their silhouettes fit this site; do not force every site into the same hall mesh.

## Lighting and atmosphere

Amber from one window; green hard key on leaves and roof. Exterior floor fog stays between 0.3 and 0.8 m. Keep the walking surface and interactable furniture readable. Unfinished backs use MAT_backstage.

## Room markers

Door inspection; return west. Each marker is a named `TRG_` empty carrying the room id. The existing `TRG_yihong_yuan_entry` is the initial marker; add specific action markers during the site pass. Game actions belong in Godot.

## Camera contract

Frame the door and foliage in a compact shot, excluding unfinished backs. Ship a 2.35:1 establishing camera and a constrained visitor rail. Check both approach and return views for exposed backs or intersections.

## Current build and acceptance

The generic hall has been replaced by a west-facing, seven-metre closed court front, paired lacquer doors, a stone threshold, low capped walls, one amber window and two banana plants with fourteen curved leaves. Four markers and three cameras are exported. Qinfang’s “Visit the red court” action follows the eastern walk; local door and foliage inspections keep the future interior closed.

The outward/return physics route and closed-door ray check pass. Desktop and portrait arrival, door and foliage captures are generated by `godot/tests/render_red_court.gd`. Source references, painted signage, foliage texture refinement and the final lighting rig/bake remain unfinished.

Acceptance: distinct site silhouette, complete specified props, named markers, traversable approach, no exposed unfinished backs on the camera rail, and one graded reference render matching this sheet. The stage-wide first pass is not evidence of completion for this site.
