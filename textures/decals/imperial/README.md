# Imperial facade title

`daguan-title.png` is original AI-generated production artwork made with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. The three Traditional Chinese characters were visually checked to read 大觀樓. This is a sign texture, not a historical reference image.

`scripts/finish_imperial_art.py` embeds the transparent gold lettering on the existing wood signboard in Blender and the glTF export. The script also gives the ten closed door leaves red lacquer and the twenty recessed panels a darker related material. The full-resolution source stays here; the Godot import uses a compressed 1024-pixel limit.

Prompt: “Create ONLY the three Traditional Chinese characters exactly ‘大觀樓’ (大 then 觀 then 樓), in one horizontal row, left to right. Bold hand-brushed regular script, accurate and clearly legible Traditional Chinese character structure, varied brush pressure with restrained dry-brush edges. Warm aged-gold ink, isolated strokes on transparent alpha. No board, paper, border, seal, signature, other marks, Latin letters, or extra characters. Wide landscape composition about 3:1, equal visual height and even spacing, generous transparent padding. Flat orthographic lettering artwork; no perspective, shadows, or lighting. This is a game sign texture, so make all three characters strong and easy to read when reduced.”
