# Bulletin hall painted art

The three PNGs in this directory are original AI-generated game textures made with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. They are production artwork, not sourced historical references. The hall title was visually checked to read 秋爽齋. `scripts/finish_study_art.py` embeds these images in the editable Blender hall and the glTF export. `scripts/configure_study_art_imports.py` sets GPU compression and a 1024-pixel runtime size limit in Godot; the full-resolution originals remain here.

Prompts:

- `hall-title.png`: “Create ONLY the three Traditional Chinese characters exactly ‘秋爽齋’ (秋 then 爽 then 齋), in one horizontal row, left to right. Bold hand-brushed regular script, visibly painted with uneven pressure and restrained dry-brush texture, accurate and legible character structure. Warm aged-gold ink, isolated strokes with genuinely transparent alpha background. No board, paper, border, seal, signature, extra marks, Latin letters, or other characters. Wide landscape composition about 3:1, equal height and even spacing, generous transparent padding. Flat orthographic artwork with no perspective, shadows, or lighting.”
- `bamboo-scroll.png`: “A vertical rice-paper scroll painted by hand: one slender bamboo stalk with a few leaves in charcoal black ink, one restrained warm-amber wash near the leaves, subtle paper fibre and worn edges. Traditional Chinese ink painting, sparse composition with generous negative space, flat orthographic front view. Tall narrow aspect ratio about 1:2.5. No calligraphy, no characters, no seals, no border, no frame, no rollers, no shadow, no perspective, no scene background. The full rectangular paper is visible and fills the image.”
- `plum-scroll.png`: “A vertical rice-paper scroll painted by hand: one slender plum branch with a few small five-petal blossoms in charcoal ink and restrained warm-amber highlights, delicate irregular brush pressure, subtle paper fibres and worn edges. Traditional Chinese ink painting, sparse composition with generous negative space, flat orthographic front view. Tall narrow aspect ratio about 1:2.5, matching a bamboo scroll beside it. No calligraphy, no characters, no seals, no border, no frame, no rollers, no shadow, no perspective, no scene background. The full rectangular paper is visible and fills the image.”
